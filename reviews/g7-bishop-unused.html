What to take away

The habit

Name what's undefended before you develop

Every miss in this game came from playing a good-looking move without a two-second check of what was loose. On moves 6, 7 and 8 the answer was the same undefended rook on a1. Developing moves are the default; they are not the answer to a specific question.

The pattern

A fianchettoed bishop is a tactical asset, not a decoration

The g7 bishop's job is the a1–h8 diagonal. When an enemy knight on c3 is the only thing in the way, Nxd5 and Bxc3+ are not sacrifices — they're deflections. Your bishop got traded off on move 11 having never once done its job.

Drill for the next ten games

Before every move in the first fifteen, say to yourself which enemy pieces are undefended and what your longest-range piece is aimed at. Nothing more elaborate than that — three of the six errors here disappear if you do it.
